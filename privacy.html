Classroom Hub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

What Classroom Hub is

Classroom Hub is a free, offline-capable web app for teachers. It helps with seating plans, points, attendance, random name picking and group making.

Information you enter

You enter class and student information yourself, such as student names, seating, points and attendance. This information is saved in your own web browser, on your own device. The app has no server of its own, and the developer does not receive, collect or store this information.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks for one permission only: access to its own private "app data" folder in your Google Drive. The app uses that folder to keep a backup copy of your classroom data so that your devices can stay in sync.

  • The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.
  • The app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts or any other Google account information.
  • The data in that folder belongs to your Google account. Only you can access it. The developer cannot see it.

Classroom Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not run advertising or analytics, and we do not track you.
  • We do not use your data for any purpose other than letting you use the app.

Student information

Student names are personal information. You are responsible for following your school's rules and the data privacy laws that apply to you. If you prefer, you can enter nicknames or student numbers instead of full names.

Deleting your data

  • On a device: use "Erase ALL data on this device" in the app's Backup tab, or clear your browser's site data.
  • In Google Drive: open Google Drive, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Classroom Hub and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
